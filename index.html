Pricing formulas: Selling price = selling_price column · Coin used per unit = coins_used ÷ quantity (for RTO/RVP rows where coins_used is blank it is rebuilt as selling_price × quantity − GMV, which matches coins_used exactly on every other row) · Coin % = coin used ÷ selling price · Cash = selling price − coin used. A “combo” is one PID at one selling price with one coin amount. DRR = net orders ÷ days live (days with orders at that combo), so combos live for only a few days are compared fairly. The best combo per PID has the highest DRR among combos whose CM% and burn/order are at least Average (not in the worst zone) with ≥ min orders; if DRRs are within the “similar DRR” % the better CM% wins.

Notes: “Days live” counts dates with at least one order at that price. Elasticity is the slope of log(orders/day) vs log(price) across a product's price points (−1.5 means a 10% price cut brings ~15% more orders/day).
